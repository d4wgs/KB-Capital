About KB Capital

KB Capital is a small, private fund that invests in prediction markets. We look for markets where the price doesn't match the odds as we see them, and we take positions when we think we have an edge.

What we do

We research events across politics, economics, sports and current affairs, then trade the outcomes on Polymarket. We aim to size positions carefully and manage risk, so no single bet decides the result.

Transparency

Our portfolio is public. The Predictions page shows our current positions, trade activity and profit and loss, updated regularly from Polymarket's public data.
