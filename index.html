What we do

A concentrated book built on directional conviction.

Finding the skew

We screen event contracts for outcomes that are heavily one-sided and close to resolution. Each candidate gets a written thesis: what has to happen, by when, and what would prove us wrong. We pass on anything we can't underwrite.

[ research image ]

Short-duration, directional

We take outright directional positions in near-dated markets, targeting one to two trades a day. Short holding periods mean fast capital turnover and less time for a thesis to be invalidated, and that turnover is what lets small gains compound.

[ trading image ]

Sized for survival

We only deploy when risk/reward favors us, size to our conviction, and stay in cash when there is no edge. Every position has a defined exit before we enter, so one bad outcome can't undo the compounding.
